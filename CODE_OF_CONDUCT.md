# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

OpenCMO contributors commit to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Be considerate, assume good intent, discuss ideas rather than people, and make space for different levels of experience and different perspectives.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person’s private information, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may clarify expectations, edit or remove contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove participants when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed in good faith and kept as confidential as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing OpenCMO.
