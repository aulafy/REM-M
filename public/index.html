Trust & safety

What AI can do

Read the public pages you request, use your project knowledge as quoted data, and prepare drafts for review.

What AI cannot do

It cannot publish without your explicit approval, relax the hard safety rules, delete your data, or connect a channel by itself.

What leaves this computer

Prompts go only to the AI endpoint you configure. Public-site checks contact those sites. Publishing sends approved content only to your chosen connector.

Where data lives

Projects, drafts, settings, and metrics are stored in the local SQLite file under your configured data folder. Secrets are masked in the UI.
