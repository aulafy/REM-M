## Product Hunt draft

**Tagline:** A local-first AI CMO that never publishes without your approval.

OpenCMO turns a business website into a strategy, content calendar, grounded drafts, and white-label client report. It runs locally, works with Ollama or compatible cloud models, and keeps a human approval boundary around every publication. v0.5 adds an agency overview, Search Console opportunities, and deterministic competitor comparisons.

## Hacker News draft

**Title:** Show HN: OpenCMO – a local-first, approval-first AI marketing workspace

I built OpenCMO for founders, freelancers, and small agencies that want useful AI assistance without handing publishing control to an agent. It is MIT-licensed, uses TypeScript/Hono/SQLite and vanilla JS, works with Ollama, and stores project data locally. The newest release adds offline white-label reports, GSC query imports, measured competitor comparisons, and multi-project agency triage. Feedback on the safety boundary and local-first workflow is especially welcome.

## Reddit draft

I have been working on an MIT-licensed local marketing workspace for people who manage several small-business clients. The design constraint is simple: AI can research and draft, but cannot publish until a person approves the exact draft. It now includes white-label offline reports, Search Console opportunity imports, competitor page checks, and a multi-project overview. I would value practical feedback from freelancers and small agencies; this is a project share, not an automated promotion.

Post only in communities whose rules permit project showcases, participate in the discussion, and do not cross-post repeatedly.
